
## Why Are Databases Useful?

1. **Data Storage**
   Store massive structured datasets that are easily searchable and retrievable.

   * Example: Ola stores customer profiles and trip histories so users can view trips later.

2. **Data Analysis**
   Analyze, summarize, and visualize data to make business decisions.

   * Example: Identify reasons for company profits or losses by analyzing sales and activity data.

3. **Record Keeping**
   Store essential records like financial transactions, customer data, and inventory.

4. **Web & Mobile Applications**
   Every dynamic app (login systems, recommendations, profiles) relies on databases.
